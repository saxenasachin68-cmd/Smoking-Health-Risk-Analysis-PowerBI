# Smoking Risk Health Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

Interactive medical analytics dashboard developed in Power BI to analyze smoking-related health risks, patient conditions, and organ health status. The dashboard provides comparative analysis between healthy and damaged organs using dynamic visualizations, patient metrics, smoking behavior insights, and healthcare risk indicators.

The project helps identify medical risk patterns associated with smoking habits, cholesterol levels, hypertension, BMI, age groups, and lifestyle behavior to support healthcare analysis and data-driven medical decisions.

---

## Medical Problem Statement

Smoking significantly affects multiple human organs and increases the risk of serious health conditions. Healthcare professionals and researchers require effective visualization tools to monitor patient conditions and evaluate smoking-related risks across different body systems.

This dashboard aims to:

- Analyze smoking behavior among patients
- Compare healthy and damaged organ conditions
- Identify high-risk patient groups
- Evaluate cholesterol and hypertension risks
- Monitor smoking duration and cigarette intake
- Improve healthcare analysis using interactive dashboards
